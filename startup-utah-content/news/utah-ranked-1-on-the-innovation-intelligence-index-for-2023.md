# Utah Ranked #1 on the Innovation Intelligence Index for 2023

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/utah-ranked-1-on-the-innovation-intelligence-index-for-2023/>

Utah has been ranked #1 on the Innovation Intelligence Index for 2023, with a score of 156.2. This prestigious recognition highlights Utah’s exceptional capabilities and performance in fostering innovation across various sectors. The state’s top ranking is a testament to robust investment in research and development, a highly skilled workforce, and a thriving ecosystem that supports technology adoption and entrepreneurial endeavors.

Key factors contributing to Utah’s leading position include strong collaboration between its universities and industries, significant growth in high-tech industries, and a high level of business competitiveness.  Utah’s commitment to education and continuous improvement in infrastructure has also played a crucial role in securing this top spot.

For more detailed insights and to explore the full 2023 Innovation Intelligence Index report, visit the official website [here](https://www.statsamerica.org/innovation2/).
