# UDOT Awarded Federal Grant to Connect Communities and Neighborhoods in St. George

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/udot-awarded-federal-grant-to-connect-communities-and-neighborhoods-in-st-george/>

_This is a summary of UDOT press release. You can read the full press release[here](https://www2.udot.utah.gov/connect/2024/03/15/udot-awarded-87-6-million-federal-grant-to-connect-communities-and-neighborhoods-in-st-george/). _

[The U.S. Department of Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/) awarded $87,618,600 to the [Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT)](https://www.udot.utah.gov/connect/) to fund local street crossings of Interstate 15 in St. George at 400 East and 900 South. 

The new crossings will provide additional connectivity of local routes on the east and west sides. 

UDOT began its final design of additional lanes on I-15 between Exits 6 and 8, where the crossings would be located.
