# Utah Innovation Fund: Elevating Utah’s Economy

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/utah-innovation-fund-elevating-utahs-economy/>

The Utah Innovation Fund (UIF) stands as a beacon of progress in Utah’s economic landscape, spearheading initiatives to propel the state to the forefront of innovation and entrepreneurship. Established by the Utah Legislature, UIF is a pivotal driver in commercializing cutting-edge technologies originating from Utah’s esteemed higher education institution. With a clear mission to support startups addressing global challenges, UIF is dedicated to nurturing promising ventures that have the potential to revolutionize industries and elevate Utah’s economic standing. 

“The Utah Innovation Fund’s objective is simple: How can we support these startups in their earliest stage that have a hard time getting funding here, so that they can stay here, get support here, and attract outside capital to Utah as opposed to the other way around,” said Gabi Tellez, the Managing Director of Utah Innovation Fund. “We can benefit from the economic development that comes from those companies staying here with their innovation and the talent pool they’ll draw.” 

## Mission and Objectives

The Fund’s core mission revolves around fostering innovation, strengthening the state’s economy, and facilitating job creation. Through strategic investments and partnerships, UIF endeavors to:

  1. **Advance Innovative Technologies**. By providing financial support and resources, the Fund aims to accelerate the development and commercialization of groundbreaking technologies originating from Utah’s educational institutions. 
  2. **Facilitate Job Creation**. By empowering startups, the Fund contributes to the creation of employment opportunities, driving economic growth and prosperity across Utah. 
  3. **Attract Entrepreneurial Talent**. UIF attempts to attract and retain entrepreneurial talent, positioning Utah as a magnet for innovation and investment. 
  4. **Ensure Financial Sustainability**. While nurturing startups, UIF remains committed to ensuring its long-term financial sustainability, thereby fostering a robust ecosystem of innovation funding within the state. 



As Utah continues its journey towards becoming a hub of innovation and entrepreneurship, the Utah Innovation Fund stands as a catalyst for change, driving forward-thinking initiatives that not only transform industries but also uplift communities and foster economic prosperity. With a steadfast commitment to supporting visionary startups and groundbreaking technologies, UIF is paving the way for a brighter, more innovative future for Utah and beyond.
