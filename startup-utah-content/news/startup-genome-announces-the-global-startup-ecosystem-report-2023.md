# Startup Genome Announces the Global Startup Ecosystem Report 2023

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/startup-genome-announces-the-global-startup-ecosystem-report-2023/>

The startup ecosystem is crucial for driving innovation, economic development, job creation and fostering a culture of entrepreneurship that benefits both local and global communities. Startup Genome has released [the Global Startup Ecosystem Report 2023 (GSER 2023)](https://startupgenome.com/report/gser2023), shedding light on the current trends of the startup ecosystem. With over a decade of analysis involving 3.5 million startups across 290 global ecosystems, this report serves as a comprehensive guide for entrepreneurs seeking insights into the current state of startup environments worldwide. 

In response to the recent economic crisis, JC Gauthier, Founder and CEO of Startup Genome, emphasized the significance of startups during the ongoing economic turbulence in his welcome message. 

“Technology doesn’t just drive economic growth and job creation, it stands to save the planet too,” said JC Gauthier. “As a community, we must continue to develop and scale innovative solutions to our pressing environmental challenges.” The GSER 2023 contains data about the impact of the recession on startups, total venture capital funding in the global startup ecosystem, and how to attract skilled talent to your ecosystem, etc. You can read the full report at [www.startupgenome.com](http://www.startupgenome.com/).
