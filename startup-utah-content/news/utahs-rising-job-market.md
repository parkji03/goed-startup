# Utah’s Rising Job Market

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/utahs-rising-job-market/>

Utah, known for its natural beauty, is becoming a hotspot for well-paying jobs. [Career.io’s](https://career.io/) recent analysis reveals that [Utah is among the least competitive markets for high-paying professions](https://career.io/career-advice/least-competitive-markets-for-highly-paid-jobs). 

Utah’s economic boom, fueled by technology, finance, and healthcare industries, makes it a promising destination for job seekers. The state’s business-friendly environment and skilled workforce attract companies, creating a strong job market. 

One standout feature is Utah’s low competition for high-paying jobs, providing professionals with better chances of landing lucrative opportunities. This advantage is particularly evident in the tech and finance sectors, where demand for skilled workers consistently surpasses available talent. 

## Key Findings:

  * In Salt Lake City, residents grew 7.8% from 2012 to 2022.
  * The city has the most competitive market, with 34.9 daily applicants for each highly-paid job. 
  * In Provo, there are just 0.1 daily applicants per high-paying job, a fraction of the candidate rate in Salt Lake City. 
  * Utah leads the market with 22.6 daily applicants per high-paying job, compared to Missouri with 19.2 daily applicants coming in second. 



Utah’s tech scene, often called “Silicon Slopes,” is booming, drawing attention for its rapid growth and innovation. Major tech companies made Utah an ideal destination for professionals seeking stability and career growth. Silicon Slopes has fueled employment growth of 56.1% since 2000. 

Utah’s emergence as the least competitive market for high-paying jobs reflects its economic strength and strategic position. As job seekers explore opportunities in a changing market, Utah stands out as a promising destination for building rewarding and well-paid careers.
