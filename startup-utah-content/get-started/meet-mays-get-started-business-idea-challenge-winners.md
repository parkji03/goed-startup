# Meet May’s Get Started: Business Idea Challenge Winners

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/meet-mays-get-started-business-idea-challenge-winners/>

Every great business starts with an idea, and the [Get Started: Business Idea Challenge](https://startup.utah.gov/turn-ideas-into-action-with-the-startup-states-new-funding-challenge/) is helping turn it into reality. Here’s a list of the May awardees who secured up to $500 in non-equity funding.

[**Breath of Life**](https://breathoflifechoking.com/) is a mom-invented, doctor-approved device designed to quickly and effectively stop choking emergencies. Portable and easy to use, it works while the victim is still sitting, unlike existing solutions that require lying down. Simple enough to be used by kids, it offers a more immediate and intuitive response to traumatic choking incidents.

**ParkPal** is a peer-to-peer marketplace that lets property owners rent out unused parking spaces to drivers seeking convenient, affordable parking near high-demand areas. This turns idle spaces into income opportunities while providing drivers with guaranteed spots. The platform also helps reduce congestion and illegal parking in busy locations.

[**PrivacyHound**](https://privacyhound.co/) is a SaaS platform that simplifies privacy compliance by identifying regulatory gaps across jurisdictions. Features like “Audit Buddy” and an integrated ticketing system deliver actionable insights seamlessly within existing workflows. The platform helps businesses reduce privacy risks, avoid fines, and justify investments amid the growing demands of AI regulation.

**Rollin ’ Harvest** is a mobile farmers market that delivers fresh, locally grown, and culturally relevant produce directly to communities. It partners with small-scale farmers through a revenue-sharing model and accepts [SNAP](https://jobs.utah.gov/customereducation/services/foodstamps/index.html) benefits to improve access to nutritious food. The initiative supports regional agriculture through a pilot pop-up market program that integrates mobility and food equity.

[**Salt City Soapworks**](https://saltcitysoapworks.com/) creates natural skincare products using unrefined Utah salt and locally sourced beeswax. Its simple, pure formulas harness the natural detoxifying power of salt while supporting local beekeepers and promoting sustainability through eco-friendly packaging. Its product line includes salt soaps, scrubs, bath bombs, shampoo bars, and beeswax balms.

[**Yoked Staff**](http://www.yokedstaff.com) is a staffing agency that connects businesses with highly skilled virtual assistants to boost productivity and support growth. It offers top-tier talent at competitive rates to help companies reduce payroll costs while enhancing work quality. The model gives business owners more time to focus on strategic growth and invest in impactful local hiring.

Apply for your chance to participate in the [Get Started: Business Idea Challenge](https://startup.utah.gov/business-idea-challenge/), or learn how to [start your business](https://startup.utah.gov/entrepreneur-journey/) and join Utah’s vibrant startup community.
