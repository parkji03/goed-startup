# Pitch Your Startup at the One Utah Summit With $50K in Cash Prizes

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/pitch-your-startup-at-the-one-utah-summit-with-50k-in-cash-prizes/>

Are you a founder in Utah? This could be your chance to turn your startup’s momentum into meaningful capital.

The [State Bank Business Challenge](https://www.suu.edu/business/entrepreneurship/state-bank-business-challenge/rules-and-entry.html), a pitch competition held during the One Utah Summit at Southern Utah University, will award $50,000 in cash prizes to winners. While programs like the [Get Started: Business Idea Challenge](https://startup.utah.gov/turn-ideas-into-action-with-the-startup-states-new-funding-challenge/) award grants for budding ideas, the State Bank Business Challenge is a competition built for early-stage businesses.

**Why Apply**

This competition offers more than capital — it delivers visibility, valuable feedback, and strategic mentoring for winners, which is why small businesses from rural and southern Utah are especially encouraged to apply. 

If you have a startup that meets the qualifications, bring your best pitch forward and prepare to step onto Utah’s entrepreneurial stage.

**What You Need to Know**

  * Eligible applicants must be legal residents of Utah.
  * Businesses must be generating revenue, not exceeding $150,000 in the prior year.
  * Applications require a 90‑second YouTube video pitch about products or services, team, revenue model, and go‑to‑market strategy.
  * Participants must attend both the semi-final and final competition in person in Cedar City. Remote participation is not allowed. 



**Timeline**

  * **Midnight, September 28** : Deadline for applications.
  * **October 1** : Notifications for semi-finalists.
  * **October 6** : In‑person semi‑final pitches at SUU.
  * **October 7** : Finalists deliver live pitches before a panel of judges.



For more details and to apply for the competition, visit the State Bank Business Challenge [website](https://www.suu.edu/business/entrepreneurship/state-bank-business-challenge/rules-and-entry.html). Learn how to move your business forward with the Startup State Initiative’s [resource page](https://startup.utah.gov/resources/) or take the first step and [start your business](https://startup.utah.gov/entrepreneur-journey/) in Utah.
