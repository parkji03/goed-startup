Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/april-newsletter/>

**Funding Sources for Utah Entrepreneurs**

Securing funding is essential for businesses or products to move from the idea stage to revenue. From grants and investors to crowdfunding and government programs, the benefits and constraints of each vary based on the business needs and models. Entrepreneurs can also consider combining funding sources to achieve sustainable growth.

Learn about the different sources of funding and what could be right for your business.

[Read More](https://startup.utah.gov/small-business-funding-finding-the-best-option-for-your-needs/)

**Savvy Productions**

[Savvy Productions](https://savvyproductions.com/), an Emmy-winning video production company, has built a strong reputation for creating high-quality corporate videos, commercials, and TV ads. Despite the challenges of entrepreneurship, founder Stephen Smith credits his success to unwavering dedication, family support, and business development programs. With numerous industry accolades, Savvy Productions thrives in Utah’s vibrant film industry, benefiting from the state’s stunning landscapes and business-friendly environment.

[Read More](https://startup.utah.gov/savvy-productions-award-winning-success-in-utahs-film-industry/)

**Baugh Entrepreneurship Institute**

[The Baugh Entrepreneurship Institute](https://www.uvu.edu/give/colleges-schools/woodbury-school-business/entrepreneurship-institute.html) at Utah Valley University’s Woodbury School of Business is a driving force for student innovation, recently strengthened by a donation from alumni Chelsea and Casey Baugh. Their support enhances programs that help students launch revenue-generating businesses, with 80% of alums starting ventures in Utah. The Baughs’ investment positions UVU as a key player in the region’s entrepreneurial ecosystem.

[Read More](https://startup.utah.gov/baugh-family-donation-fuels-next-generation-of-entrepreneurs-at-uvu/)

Using company data to make informed decisions can be a strong advantage for startups and entrepreneurs aiming for growth. By tracking key metrics such as user engagement, sales, client retention rate, and more, you can make smarter choices and reduce uncertainty. But it’s not just about the numbers — your instincts, what you’re seeing in the market, and your own experience are all part of the equation. You’ll be better equipped to make the right decisions and keep moving forward.

– Brad Bonham, Utah’s Entrepreneur-In-Residence
