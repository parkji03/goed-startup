Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/may-newsletter/>

**Outsmart the Summer Slowdown While Others Hit Pause**

This time of year marks the beginning of a seasonal shift, and for many businesses, that can mean a slower pace. But for those willing to take a strategic approach, this can be a valuable opportunity to plan, refocus, and position for growth.

Whether your business skills need sharpening or it’s been some time since reviewing your company goals and financials, learn how to stay proactive as summer approaches. Take advantage of the momentum you gained while others slow down.

[Read More](https://startup.utah.gov/how-to-outsmart-the-summer-slowdown-and-grow-your-business/)

**Anatomy Boxing**

[Anatomy Boxing](https://www.anatomyboxing.com/), a woman-owned gym in Bountiful, was founded by Julia Misrasi to create an inclusive, community-focused space where people of all skill levels can build confidence, discipline, and strength through boxing, fitness, and self-defense programs. She emphasizes resilience, financial preparation, and perseverance as key lessons from her entrepreneurial journey.

Learn how Misrasi turned her passion into reality while balancing life as a single mom and full-time recruiter, driven by a deep belief in her mission and a strong support system.

[Read More](https://startup.utah.gov/anatomy-boxing-building-strength-confidence-and-community/)

**The Mill at SLCC**

[The Mill](https://themillatslcc.com/) at Salt Lake Community College is a vital resource in Utah’s entrepreneurial landscape. In addition to offering workspace, it connects entrepreneurs with mentorship, funding opportunities, educational workshops, and a supportive community, helping refine their ideas and scale. The Mill has launched and seen companies to success, showcasing the strong, intentional support available to Utah’s business community.

[Read More](https://startup.utah.gov/growing-utahs-next-generation-of-businesses-at-the-mill/)

**Gratitude and Well Wishes to Our Entrepreneur-In-Residence**

Please join us in thanking [Brad Bonham](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bradedisonbonham/), Utah’s first-ever entrepreneur-in-residence, for his years of dedication and contributions to the Governor’s Office of Economic Opportunity.

Brad oversaw the development and launch of the Startup State Initiative after visiting with hundreds of entrepreneurs and business leaders across the state. Every aspect of the initiative has been designed to support Utah’s founders by supercharging Utah’s entrepreneur ecosystem and making Utah the Startup Capital of the World. Brad’s expertise, insight, and vitality helped establish the foundation for this initiative’s growth and success. We’re deeply grateful for his leadership and wish him all the best as he launches his newest venture.
