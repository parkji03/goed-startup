**PinPark**

PinPark streamlines the parking experience by allowing drivers to reserve spots in real time, eliminating the stress of circling crowded lots and reducing traffic congestion. Behind the scenes, its data-driven automation helps parking lot operators monitor availability, forecast demand, and boost revenue. Designed for universities, hospitals, ski resorts, and other high-traffic destinations, PinPark delivers a seamless, efficient system that benefits drivers and property managers alike.

[Read More](https://startup.utah.gov/pinparks-mission-to-make-parking-more-efficient/)

**Larry H. & Gail Miller Center for Entrepreneurship**

Southern Utah University’s Larry H. & Gail Miller Center for Entrepreneurship equips students and local founders with the tools, mentorship, and space to launch and grow their ventures. From providing access to pitch competitions like the State Bank Business Challenge at the One Utah Summit to weekly Speaker Series and hands-on prototyping labs, the center offers courses, networks, and real-world resources that turn early-stage ideas into thriving businesses.

[Read More](https://startup.utah.gov/suu-miller-center-for-entrepreneurship-fuels-big-ambitions-for-students/)
