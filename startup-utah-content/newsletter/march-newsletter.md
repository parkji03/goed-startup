**Sunshine Buns**

Rooted in a 45-year-old family recipe, [Sunshine Buns](https://sunshinebuns.com/) is a cinnamon roll venture inspired by co-founder Chrisi Hammer’s mother, Mema, who spent decades perfecting the recipe. It began in 2022 with online sales and deliveries before opening its first storefront in Orem. The company quickly gained traction, selling out within minutes after launching on Instagram. The four founders built Sunshine Buns through grassroots efforts and mentorship, crediting Utah’s strong startup ecosystem for their success.

[Read More](https://startup.utah.gov/how-sunshine-buns-grew-from-a-family-recipe-to-a-thriving-business/)

**iHub**

[The Innovation Hub of Utah](https://www.ihubutah.org/) is more than a coworking space — it’s a collaborative community. In addition to mentorship from experienced business leaders, iHub fosters a culture of shared growth, with founders exchanging insights, resources, and encouragement. This dynamic environment creates lasting connections, driving innovation through teamwork and mutual success. Providing free office space, networking opportunities, and access to capital, iHub ensures startups receive support while contributing to a thriving ecosystem that fuels entrepreneurship.

[Read More](https://startup.utah.gov/ihub-utah-empowering-the-next-generation-of-entrepreneurs/)
