# Contact

![Startup State logo white](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/startup-logo-small.svg)

If you have comments, questions, feedback, or suggestions, we want to hear from you.

Please email us at [business@utah.gov](mailto:business@utah.gov), and a member of our team will get back to you as quickly as possible.

We encourage you to follow us on social media @BusinessUtah, to use #StartupState, and to [subscribe to our GOEO email newsletter](https://cdn.forms-content.sg-form.com/06b418c5-1057-11ee-9a80-ca5180dad175).
