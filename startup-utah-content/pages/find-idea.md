## FIND YOUR BIG IDEA

### How do you want to change the world?

![Find your big idea icon](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/idea-icon.svg)

Starting a business can be overwhelming, but you're not alone. Utahns start successful businesses daily, from restaurants to agriculture to e-commerce shops. Whether you're in the heart of Salt Lake City, the red rocks of Saint George, or the snow of northern Utah, we’re here to help you find what you need for your small business.

Let's explore how you can kickstart your entrepreneurial journey.

[Full Resource List](https://startup.utah.gov/resources/)

#### Brainstorming Ideas

Every great business starts with an idea. Here are some resources to help you evaluate your idea before moving forward. These resources cover ways to identify winning ideas and where to go if you need support.

  * [Small Business Development Centers  opens in a new tab](https://utahsbdc.org/) \- **SBDC Network**   
Small Business Development Centers offer free 1:1 consulting to help you find the right business idea.
  * [Startup Business Ideas: Four Steps to Identify the Right One  opens in a new tab](https://www.sba.gov/blog/startup-business-ideas-4-steps-identify-right-one) \- **Small Business Administration**   
This resource can help you see if you're on the right track.
  * [8 Easy Businesses to Start](https://www.sba.gov/blog/8-easy-businesses-start) \- **Small Business Administration**   
8 business ideas from the SBA.  opens in a new tab
  * [What Business Should I Start? 6 Factors to Help You Decide  opens in a new tab](https://www.uschamber.com/co/start/startup/deciding-what-business-to-start) \- **U.S. Chamber of Commerce**   
An article from the U.S. Chamber of Commerce.

#### How to Monetize Your Passion

Many people want to monetize an existing passion, hobby, or skill. The following resources will help you understand some of the steps required to grow your passion into a profitable small business.

  * [How to Realistically Make Money from Your Passion  opens in a new tab](https://www.entrepreneur.com/leadership/how-to-realistically-make-money-from-your-passion/378275) \- **Entrepreneur **  
Learn how to monetize the things you care about.
  * [Learn Business Skills to Monetize Your Passion  opens in a new tab](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/turn-your-passion-profession-learn-monetize-skill-create-ritu-negi) \- **LinkedIn**  
Learn skills like accounting, marketing, and sales to take your passion or hobby to the next level.
