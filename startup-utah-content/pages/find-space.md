## Find office space

### Connect with other entrepreneurs, businesspeople, and professional associations for support, advice, and potential partnerships.

![icon of a person on a laptop](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/office_space_purple.svg)

Finding the right office space is essential for entrepreneurs. It provides a dedicated environment conducive to focus and productivity. A well-suited office space energizes the business, creating a positive work atmosphere that contributes to overall growth and success. Options include traditional office space leases and coworking spaces. 

[Full Resource List](https://startup.utah.gov/resources/)

#### Coworking Spaces

Coworking spaces play a crucial role in supporting small businesses and entrepreneurship communities by providing cost-effective and flexible work environments. These spaces encourage networking, collaboration, and resource-sharing, fostering a dynamic ecosystem that promotes innovation and growth among diverse entrepreneurs.

#### Utah’s Commercial Real Estate Data

Resimplifi has been selected to serve as Utah’s real estate research partner, tasked with identifying, cataloging, and validating all available commercial real estate in the state. This comprehensive database will be accessible to site selectors, corporate real estate managers, businesses, brokers, and developers, fostering transparency and economic growth.
