## Relocate Your Business to Utah

### Utah offers competitive tax incentives and other benefits to businesses interested in relocating to Utah.

![Relocate your business to Utah icon](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/step-16-icon-blue.svg)

Utah provides attractive tax incentives and benefits for businesses considering relocation, contributing to the state's booming economy. With a fast-growing economy, exceptional quality of life, a skilled workforce, and a community-oriented mindset, Utah is recognized as a prime location for starting a business. The state's business-friendly environment, coupled with various support programs, positions it as an ideal destination for entrepreneurs seeking opportunities for growth and success. Exploring Utah's competitive advantages can be a strategic move for businesses looking to thrive in a dynamic and supportive economic landscape.

#### Business Incentives

Whether growing from within or recruiting targeted industry firms, Utah’s economy continues to lead the nation with its business-friendly environment. The Governor’s Office of Economic Opportunity offers financial incentives for qualifying local and out-of-state companies seeking to expand or relocate to Utah.
