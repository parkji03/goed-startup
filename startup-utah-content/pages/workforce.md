## Workforce and Talent Acquisition

### Learn more about how your business can find the top talent in Utah.

![icon with three people in a line](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/workforce-talent-aquisition-blue.svg)

Investing in the workforce enhances productivity, employee satisfaction, and overall business success.

There are many resources available to support businesses in offering competitive salaries, providing opportunities for skill development, and fostering a positive work environment to attract and retain top talent.

[Full Resource List](https://startup.utah.gov/resources/)

* * *

#### Supporting Workforce Acquisition

Public and private organizations play a vital role in supporting small businesses by providing assistance in attracting top talent. They offer valuable insights, job-matching services, and workforce development programs that help small businesses identify and connect with skilled individuals, contributing to their recruitment efforts and overall success.

  * [Department of Workforce Services](https://jobs.utah.gov/)
  * [Talent Ready Utah](https://talentready.ushe.edu/)
