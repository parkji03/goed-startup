## **Write your Business Plan**

### Create a clear plan outlining your business goals, target market, and how you will achieve success.

![Write your business plan icon](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/step-6-icon-purple.svg)

Writing a business plan for your small business involves outlining your goals, target market, and operational strategy. It's a crucial roadmap that helps attract investors, secure loans, and guide your business decisions.

There are several different types of business plans in the market. The lean startup model emphasizes a streamlined approach, focusing on essential elements to avoid unnecessary complexity while traditional models are more thorough and detailed.

All business plan structures help you articulate your business’s unique value proposition, identify products and services, conduct market research, develop a marketing strategy, and build business financials.

A well-crafted business plan communicates your vision to customers, building trust and credibility, while also serving as a flexible tool to adapt and grow your business efficiently.

#### Lean Business Plan

Lean business planning is a flexible way to create a simple roadmap for your business. It helps you focus on what's crucial and allows for quick changes as your business grows. Unlike traditional plans that can be long and detailed, lean planning is about staying nimble and adapting easily.

Learn about the four essential parts of a lean business plan.

#### Traditional Business Plan

Traditional business plans are thorough documents that cover everything about your business. They're crucial for loans and investors but can be more rigid compared to lean plans, which are simpler and better for adapting to changes.
