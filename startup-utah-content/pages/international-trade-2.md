## International Trade

### Expand your business by looking overseas for valuable revenue streams and important business relationships.

![International trade icon](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/step-15-icon-blue.svg)

Expanding through international trade is an excellent way for businesses to access new markets and grow. Small businesses can tap into global markets, benefit from government support, and explore opportunities that contribute to increased revenue and long-term success. Understanding the basics of international trade can be a game-changer for businesses seeking to broaden their horizons and establish a presence on the global stage.

#### **Protect Your Internation Intellectual Property**

Intellectual property protection is regional and country-specific. 

  *  [United States Patent & Trademark Office (USPTO) IP Policy](https://www.uspto.gov/ip-policy) includes information on international engagement, policies, and treaties.
  *  [World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)](https://www.wipo.int/portal/en/) – WIPO is the United Nations agency that serves the world’s inventors.
  *  Five largest intellectual property offices, known as the IP5: 

 -  [China National Intellectual Property Administration](https://english.cnipa.gov.cn/)

 -  [European Patent Office](https://www.epo.org/en)

 -  [Japan Patent Office](https://www.jpo.go.jp/e/)

 -  [Korean Intellectual Property Office](https://www.kipo.go.kr/en/)

 -  [United States Patent & Trademark Office](https://www.uspto.gov/)[s in a ne](https://www.wtcutah.com/)
