## Growth Stage Funding

### Secure the necessary financial resources to scale your business by attracting investors or exploring funding options suited for your growth stage.

![Growth stage funding icon](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/step-12-icon-blue.svg)

Capital is essential to growth-stage businesses. Whether it's loans, equity investment, or other funding sources, knowing the available options helps companies make informed decisions about how to fuel their growth. This knowledge empowers business owners to choose the most suitable and sustainable financing strategies, aligning with their specific needs and goals.

[Full Resource List](https://startup.utah.gov/resources/)

#### Preparing to Pitch to Investors

When pursuing growth stage funding, businesses should refine their pitches by clearly articulating their unique value proposition, honing their brand identity, and crafting a compelling pitch deck and story. This strategic refinement not only attracts potential investors but also demonstrates a solid understanding of their market positioning, increasing the likelihood of securing the necessary funding for expansion.

  * [Pitch Progams](https://kinectcapital.org/pitch-program/) \- **Kinect Capital**

#### Grants and government funding

Government grants for small businesses offer financial support without the need for repayment. To qualify, businesses often need a well-defined proposal, detailed budget, and a clear demonstration of how the funds will be used to benefit the community or achieve specific goals. These grants can be an excellent option for businesses looking for non-repayable financial assistance to kickstart or enhance their operations.

  * [Guide to Applying for Grants](https://issuu.com/go-utah/docs/goeo-guide-to-applying-for-grants) \- **Governor ’s Office of Economic Opportunity**

Business competitions offer funding opportunities for small businesses through contests where entrepreneurs pitch their ideas to win financial support. #### CrowdFunding

Crowdfunding allows small businesses to raise funds by presenting their ideas to a wide audience. Utilizing these crowdfunding options provides an avenue for businesses to access capital directly from supporters, turning their business ideas into reality.

#### Small Business Loans

Small business loans can help fund business needs like startup costs, expansion, or working capital.

To get a business loan, you typically need a solid business plan, good credit history, clear financial projections, and a few years of business experience. Businesses seeking to grow, invest in equipment, or manage cash flow may consider a loan as a viable option to meet their financial objectives.

  * [List of banks in Utah  opens in a new tab](https://dfi.utah.gov/financial-institutions/banks/) \- **Department of Financial Institutions**

#### Angel Investors

Angel investor funding involves individuals providing capital to small businesses in exchange for ownership equity. To attract angel investors, businesses should have a compelling business idea, a solid plan, and a clear strategy for achieving growth. Meeting these requirements enhances the likelihood of securing angel investor funding to support and scale your small business.

  * [Park City Angels opens in a new tab](https://www.parkcityangels.com/)
  * [SLC Angels opens in a new tab](https://www.slcangels.com/)

#### Venture Capital

Venture capital funding involves investors providing capital to small businesses in exchange for equity. To attract venture capital, businesses should prepare a compelling pitch, demonstrate high growth potential, and have a solid business plan outlining how the investment will be used to scale the company. Understanding and meeting these requirements increases the chances of securing venture capital funding for your small business.
