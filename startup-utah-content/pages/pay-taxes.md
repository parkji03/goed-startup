## Pay Your Taxes

![Utah capitol icon](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/step-10-icon-purple.svg)

Paying taxes in Utah involves working with the Utah State Tax Commission. To determine what you owe, check the state's tax guidelines and use their online resources or consult with a tax professional. The Utah State Tax Commission provides accessible information and resources to help small business owners understand their tax obligations and promote compliance. Utilizing these resources ensures you're informed and equipped to fulfill your tax responsibilities in Utah.

  * [Tax Information for Small Businesses  opens in a new tab](https://tax.utah.gov/business) \- **Utah State Tax Commission**
