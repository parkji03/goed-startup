## Strategic Planning for Growth

### Learn more about how your business can plan for growth

![graph with three bars increasing and an arrow pointing upwards](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/strategic-planning-growth-blue.svg)

As small businesses reach significant milestones, strategic planning for growth becomes imperative. This includes investing in leadership training to equip founders with essential management skills, learning how to effectively manage employees, and scaling systems to accommodate increased demands. By prioritizing growth planning, small businesses can navigate expansion successfully.

#### Leadership Training

Investing in leadership training cultivates strong, capable leaders who can navigate challenges, inspire teams, and drive organizational success. Well-trained leaders contribute to a positive work culture, effective decision-making, and overall business resilience, fostering long-term growth and adaptability in a competitive market.

  * [Clifton Strengths Assessement for Teams](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/252137/home.aspx)
  * [Thrive Leadership Training](https://www.sba.gov/sba-learning-platform/thrive-emerging-leaders-reimagined) \- Small Business Administration
