## Join a Community

### Connect with other entrepreneurs, businesspeople, and professional associations for support, advice, and potential partnerships.

![Join a community icon](https://startup.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/step-11-icon.svg)

Joining a community, such as a local chamber of commerce or industry network, is key for a small business. It's like having a supportive team that helps you learn the ropes and avoid pitfalls. Being part of these networks means you're in the loop about what's happening in your industry and community. Plus, you can meet other business owners, share ideas, and find new customers. These communities often offer workshops and events that can teach practical skills, connect you with mentors, and make your business journey less overwhelming. It helps you build friendships and connections that support you as you navigate the world of small business.
