# Discover What’s New for Startups at the One Utah Summit

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/discover-whats-new-for-startups-at-the-one-utah-summit/>

It’s not too late to attend the One Utah Summit, which will take place at Southern Utah University on October 7-9, 2024. This event brings together business leaders, entrepreneurs, and innovators to discuss economic opportunities in Utah.

A highlight of the summit is the State Bank Business Challenge, where Utah’s top entrepreneurs pitch ideas to win cash, gain exposure, and receive mentorship. Attendees can watch the semi-finals on October 7 as judges determine who will move on to compete for $50,000 in cash prizes. You can vote for the $5,000 People’s Choice Award during the final round the next day.

Entrepreneur-in-Residence Brad Edison Bonham will also take the stage to discuss “What’s Happening in Utah: The Startup Capital of the World”. He’ll share the latest updates on the Startup State Initiative, offering insights into Utah’s rapid growth as a premier place for launching and scaling businesses, with key takeaways for entrepreneurs.

From networking with industry leaders to gaining practical strategies, inspiration, and insights, the One Utah Summit is a must-attend event for entrepreneurs looking to expand their business skills and connections.

Go to [oneutahsummit.utah.gov/tickets](https://oneutahsummit.utah.gov/tickets/) to register.
