# Hale Centre Theatre: Building Community Culture and Economic Growth

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/hale-centre-theatre-building-community-culture-and-economic-growth/>

Renowned for its world-class performances, [Hale Centre Theatre](https://www.hct.org/Online/default.asp) has been a pillar of Utah’s culture, economy, and community for over 40 years. What started as a small, family-run theater has grown into a thriving enterprise, welcoming over 600,000 patrons each year and making a lasting impact on the local arts scene.

In this video spotlight of Hale Centre Theatre, watch as co-founders Mark and Sally Dietlein reflect on their journey from modest beginnings to becoming a cornerstone of Utah’s cultural landscape. They discuss the theater’s evolution, emphasizing the importance of community support and the challenges of managing rapid growth. The Dietleins highlight the journey that led to the theater’s significant economic contributions to the state — an impact of over $42 million in 2023. They share insights for aspiring entrepreneurs, exemplifying with their story how dedication and community engagement can transform an idea into a thriving venture.

Hale Centre Theatre has set the stage for businesses in the arts to thrive and grow in Utah. Are you ready to bring your vision to life? Visit [startup.utah.gov](http://startup.utah.gov) to learn how to launch your business.
