# Zia Zensations Brings Unique Essential Oil Blends to Utah’s Wellness Scene

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/zia-zensations-brings-unique-essential-oil-blends-to-utahs-wellness-scene/>

Zia Zensations, a niche aromatherapy purveyor, has spent the last seven years working its way through the Utah market space. In addition to its high-quality oils, it offers various natural aroma-based products that aim to promote physical health and mental wellness. 

At 17 years old, Joe Zia — who cofounded the company with his wife, Esther — moved from the U.S. to Costa Rica, where he launched a tech and video game company. Although he gained invaluable experience, the business wasn’t ultimately aligned with Joe’s passion for health.

After nearly 30 years in Costa Rica, where the couple spent much of their time learning and practicing zen philosophy, martial arts, and focusing on personal well-being, Joe and Esther decided it was time to move somewhere new — Utah.

Their love of the state first formed when they visited in 2009 to see family. During their trip, the couple “fell in love with the friendly people, their steadfast values, and the awesome natural beauty.” 

Not long after they made the move, the Zias began to look into local wellness products, which led them to identify that prices for premium-quality oils were high, prompting them to fill the gap in the market.

After identifying the complexity in essential oils and wellness, the company’s goal to “bring all of the benefits of aromatherapy to everyone through unrelenting quality at a fair price” has led to its creation of a line of pure essential oils and premium perfumes that offer various health benefits as well as create delightful sensory experiences.

To further cater to consumer needs, Zia Zensations even offers custom compositions per request.

As stated on its [website](https://ziazensations.com/), “Zia Zensations aims to simplify and destigmatize essential oils and show everyone how easy and beneficial it is to use all-natural plant-based products.” To succeed in this mission, Zia boasts unrivaled transparency, knowledgeable staff, and great customer service while holding steadfast to its commitment to sustainability, innovation, and excellence.

“At Zia Zensations we are fully aware that helping people improve their emotional and physical being will contribute to a better world.”

As for fellow entrepreneurs, Joe advises to “plan and budget carefully. Preview all of the steps beforehand and start out lean.”

Learn more about [Zia Zensations](https://ziazensations.com/) online or check out its products by visiting its physical store, which is located at The Shops at South Town in Sandy.
