# Networking: How Events Bolster Your Business and Grow Community

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/networking-how-events-bolster-your-business-and-grow-community/>

Effective networking is one of the most powerful tools an entrepreneur can have. Whether building support from fellow entrepreneurs, attracting investors, or connecting with customers, a strong network can be the difference between momentum and stagnation.

Fortunately for Utah’s founders, the state offers a wide array of groups, gatherings, and events to help foster these vital connections. From weekly meetups to annual conferences, here are some great resources to grow your entrepreneurial network.

[**One Utah Summit**](https://oneutahsummit.utah.gov/)**** brings together leaders from the government, local businesses, and the broader community to advance economic opportunity across the state. Held each fall, the summit features keynote speakers, industry panels, and networking sessions tailored to Utah’s entrepreneurs and changemakers. With a strong focus on innovation, collaboration, and statewide impact, it offers a unique space to share ideas, explore solutions, and connect with those shaping Utah’s future.

[**1 Million Cups**](https://startup.utah.gov/unlock-connections-and-ideas-at-orems-1-million-cups/) is a weekly gathering that helps entrepreneurs pitch ideas, get feedback, and connect with peers. Held every Wednesday morning, the meetings begin with brief introductions, followed by two entrepreneur pitches and open networking to foster building relationships. Whether sharing a new venture or simply showing up to support and learn, each session provides opportunities to make meaningful connections.

[**Silicon Slopes Summit**](https://startup.utah.gov/silicon-slopes-empowering-startups-and-fueling-innovation/) is one of the state’s largest gatherings for entrepreneurs, innovators, and tech leaders. With thousands of attendees and high-profile speakers, it offers opportunities to share ideas, learn from top companies, and build professional relationships. Events like Startup Alley allow emerging founders to showcase ventures and even meet investors, helping them take the next big step.

[**Third Thursday @ Three**](https://startup.utah.gov/third-thursday-three-is-where-and-when-to-pitch-your-startup/) is a monthly event for entrepreneurs, investors, and startup enthusiasts. Hosted at Kiln Lehi, it features five fast-paced startup pitches followed by open networking, making it a great place to find potential collaborators, mentors, or investors. Whether sharing an idea or exploring the scene, Third Thursday @ Three connects you with Utah’s vibrant and growing startup ecosystem.

[**Utah Tech Week**](https://startup.utah.gov/celebrate-utahs-thriving-tech-scene-at-utah-tech-week/) is a prime opportunity for founders, investors, and professionals to grow their networks within Utah and celebrate its thriving tech scene. The annual event features dozens of independently hosted panels, workshops, and gatherings, each designed to spark collaboration and “happy collisions” among attendees. Highlights include a high-stakes pitch competition and the Night Market, where local vendors and the community will come together to connect, celebrate, and grow.

These are just a few of Utah’s many events and gatherings that help to build community and foster connections. To find other offerings, visit the Startup State Initiative’s [resource page](https://startup.utah.gov/resources/), or learn how to [start your business](https://startup.utah.gov/entrepreneur-journey/) in Utah.
