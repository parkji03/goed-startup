# Empowering Utah Entrepreneurs with SBDC Entrepreneur Academy

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/empowering-utah-entrepreneurs-with-sbdc-entrepreneur-academy/>

Utah’s[ Small Business Development Centers (SBDC)](https://online.utahsbdc.org/home) support entrepreneurs through all stages of the startup process. This vital resource provides expert guidance, networking opportunities, training, podcasts, webinars, and resource access.

The Entrepreneur Academy is a central resource of the Utah SBDC which offers seven specialized business essentials e-counseling modules, with two additional modules coming soon. This program is available for free and empowers entrepreneurs with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to start and build a successful business. 

## [Forming Your Business](https://online.utahsbdc.org/course/businessformation) 

Explore the different business structures and select the one that best fits the company, goals, and industry. Learn how to register a business with the Department of Commerce and become licensed with the city or county.

## [Financing Your Business](https://online.utahsbdc.org/course/financing-your-business)

Prepare to navigate securing capital for an idea or business. Learn about the various sources of capital, how to prepare for lender meetings, and the government programs and support available in the state of Utah.

## [Marketing Your Business](https://online.utahsbdc.org/course/marketing-for-your-business) 

Follow a step-by-step guide to create a comprehensive marketing plan, conduct market research, and leverage government support. Gain the confidence to navigate the world of marketing effectively.

## [Managing Your Employees](https://online.utahsbdc.org/course/managing-your-employees)

Determine the type of employment hierarchy, create a system for hiring and onboarding staff, and decide when to outsource. Learn the best practices for employment management and retention strategies.

## [E-Commerce for Your Business ](https://online.utahsbdc.org/course/e-commerce-for-your-business)

Gain strategic insights into establishing and growing an online business in Utah. Learn how to research, create, and develop an e-commerce business successfully.

## [Cybersecurity for Small Business](https://online.utahsbdc.org/course/cybersecurity) 

Understand the most common forms of cyberattacks and the real risks they pose to small businesses in Utah. Prepare to equip a company with the tools and knowledge to defend against these threats.

## [Managing Your Customers](https://online.utahsbdc.org/course/crm-strategies) 

Create a customer-centered business and naturally forge emotional connections with customers. Discover affordable software solutions that support this goal.

With these e-counseling courses available to the public for free, the SBDC is dedicated to supporting Utah entrepreneurs through all phases of the startup process and creating an environment of success in the Utah startup ecosystem.
