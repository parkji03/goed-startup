# How the Startup State Chatbot Can Assist You

Source: <https://startup.utah.gov/how-the-startup-state-chatbot-can-assist-you/>

The Startup State Initiative offers a wide range of resources, from a statewide [resource list](https://startup.utah.gov/resources/) to its [entrepreneur journey](https://startup.utah.gov/entrepreneur-journey/) that outlines the startup process step by step. A key feature of its site is Nova, an interactive chatbot accessible from the bottom corner of the screen, appearing as a green chat bubble.

Designed to be user-friendly, Nova assists users who may be unsure of their next steps — whether navigating the site or progressing in their startup journey.

When a conversation is initiated, Nova will collect preliminary information, such as the stage a business is in, which industry it most closely aligns with and where it is located. Once it has a feel for the company and product or service, you can begin to ask questions.

For example, if you’re interested in funding options, Nova might respond by saying, “Funding sources include small business loans, grants, venture capital, angel investors, business competitions, and crowdfunding. Check out our [small business funding page](https://startup.utah.gov/fund-small-business/) for more information.” It may also link to reated events or share filtered resources.

Unlike generative AI models, Nova pulls from a curated database of information tailored to common startup inquiries. Nova can also direct users to relevant resources within the website or external sources for further guidance if a question falls outside its scope.

Nova serves as a bridge between entrepreneurs and the vast array of resources offered by the Startup State Initiative, making starting and growing a business more accessible and navigable. As an additional fact, users can also ask Nova questions, like what its favorite food is.
